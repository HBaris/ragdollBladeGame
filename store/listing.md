## English

**Name / title:** Ragdoll Kılıç

**App Store subtitle:** Jelly ragdoll sword duels

**Google Play short description:** Wobbly ragdoll sword duels: slice, swing on ropes, topple titans by the neck!

**App Store promotional text:** No menus: open, run, slice. Wobbly jelly fighters, flying limbs, and giant titans you topple by swinging in on a rope and cutting the neck.

**App Store keywords:** ragdoll,sword,titan,duel,physics,jelly,battle,rope,horse,slice,samurai,arena,stickman

**Full description:**

No menus, no waiting: open the game, drag your finger, slice.

Ragdoll Kılıç is a physics sword-fighting game with wobbly jelly warriors. One finger both runs and swings the sword: push a little to turn, push more to run, flick fast to SLICE. Arms, legs and heads fly; a fighter who loses a leg keeps hopping, and one who loses both keeps crawling.

• Physics ragdolls: every hit is different, every fall is funny.
• Dismemberment: cut a leg and they hop, cut both and they crawl.
• Titans: swing on ropes and topple giant titans by the neck with dual blades. Back off when they vent steam!
• Titan battle: fight three titans alongside rope-flying and mounted allies.
• Mounted battle: drift your horse into a whole army.
• Dark grid arena: rising tiles and waves launch you into the air.
• Rope platform: cut the ropes, the platform tilts, everyone slides.
• Offline, no ads, no data collection.

Contains cartoon violence.
